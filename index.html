About the host institution

Engineering Excellence. Inspiring Innovation.

S.A. Engineering College was established by the Dharma Naidu Educational and Charitable Trust in 1998–99. The institution is located in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, and is affiliated with Anna University. The college has AICTE approval and attained autonomous status in 2019–20.

The institution provides academic facilities, experienced faculty, and an environment designed to support quality education and research. The college offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across engineering and related disciplines.

About the conference

Technology with Purpose. Innovation with Impact.

The International Conference on Smart Technologies for Sustainable Development Goals provides a collaborative platform for researchers, academics, industry experts, and professionals to explore smart technological solutions to global challenges.

Aligned with the United Nations 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, ICSTSDG encourages research that promotes economic growth, social equity, environmental protection, and long-term prosperity. The conference focuses on interdisciplinary research, emerging technologies, and innovative approaches that support a sustainable future.

Emerging Technologies

Machine learning, IoT, cloud and edge computing, robotics, and secure systems applied to real engineering problems.

Sustainable Innovation

Green computing, renewable technologies, energy-efficient electronics, and resource-conscious manufacturing.

Global Research Collaboration

A shared platform for academics, industry experts, and professionals, in collaboration with IEEE Madras Section chapters.

For authors

Author Guidelines

Summary of the guidance published on the 2026 conference page.

  • Format. Standard two-column IEEE conference format, Times New Roman 10-point, with strict margin settings.
  • Length. Usually limited to 4–6 pages including figures and references.
  • Title and authors. A clear, concise title, followed by author names, affiliations and email addresses.
  • Abstract and keywords. A one-paragraph abstract of 150–250 words covering problem, method and results, plus 3–5 relevant keywords.
  • Structure. Typically Introduction, Methodology, Results and Conclusion.
  • Figures, tables, equations. Clearly labelled and referenced in the text; equations properly formatted and numbered.
  • References. IEEE numbered citation style.
  • Language and originality. Clear, formal English; the work must be original.

Check before you write

The 2026 page states 4–6 pages, while other IEEE conferences may set different limits. Verify the exact requirements for your submission with the organisers.

Submission

Submission Guidelines

The original 2026 submission deadlines have passed. The workflow below describes how the 2026 edition handled submissions.

  1. Prepare an original manuscript

    The paper must not be under review or published elsewhere.

  2. Format it for the conference

    Follow the applicable IEEE conference instructions.

  3. Review your work

    Check originality, correctness, clarity and relevance to a track.

  4. Submit through the portal

    Upload an IEEE-compliant PDF with embedded fonts and complete metadata. Blind review may require removing author identities.

  5. Respond to reviews

    Papers are reviewed on novelty, technical quality, relevance and clarity.

  6. Prepare the camera-ready paper

    If accepted, revise according to reviewer comments and meet all IEEE formatting requirements.

  7. Complete formalities

    Submit the IEEE copyright form and register. At least one author must register.

  8. Present the paper

    An accepted paper must be presented to be included in the proceedings.
